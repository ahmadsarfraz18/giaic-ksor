This is a *sandbox* Knowledge System of Record: a minimal learning and
practice record for testing KSoR mechanics, the MCP tools they serve, and
document governance workflows. Everything here is practice material — sample
technical notes and practice policies written to exercise the mechanics,
nothing more.

## What belongs here

- Practice technical notes used to test document governance and search.
- Practice policies used to test the approval and takedown workflows.
- Any other sample documents the owner writes to learn how the mechanics work.

The audience for every document is `public`.

## What does not belong here

- Real company policies or business data.
- Production code, credentials, or secrets.
- Private operational knowledge.
- Broad world knowledge or general trivia.

When a question is not covered by this record, the correct answer is
**"not in this corpus"** — never a guess drawn from outside the record.
