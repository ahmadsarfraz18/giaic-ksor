## Return window

Customers may return an item within **30 days** of purchase. A valid receipt is
required at the time of return.

## Refund processing

Once a return is accepted, the refund is issued within **5 working days**.

## Item condition

Items must be **unused** and in their original condition to qualify for a
refund.

## Faulty goods

Faulty or defective items are **exempt from the 30-day return window**.
Customers may return faulty goods at any time under applicable consumer
protection law.
