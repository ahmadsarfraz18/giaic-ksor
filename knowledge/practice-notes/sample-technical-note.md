This is a practice technical note. It exists to exercise KSoR mechanics: how a
document moves from draft to stable, how a summary is attached, and how a claim
cites its source.

## What a technical note looks like

A technical note records a practice observation or a how-to, written by the
owner for the sandbox. It says what was tried, what was observed, and
what the reader should take away.

## Citing a source

A claim that carries a number or a fact names its source and cites it from the
body[^scope]. When the record changes, the note is updated to match — never
the other way around.

[^scope]: The sandbox scope, `knowledge/sandbox-scope.md`, 2026-09-23.
